Frequently Asked Questions about Incompany Training

? What are the benefits of incompany training versus open registration?

With incompany training, your staff learns in their familiar work environment, utilizing your own emergency equipment, escape corridors, and specific facility layout. You also save on travel time and costs, while benefiting from substantial group discounts from 6+ attendees.

? What requirements must our training room meet?

For BHV or First Aid, we need a quiet room with sufficient tables and chairs for theory. For firefighting practice, a safe outdoor space (such as a 6x6m courtyard or parking lot) is sufficient. We bring all simulators, manikins, AEDs, and eco-friendly extinguishers.

? Can we flexibly schedule dates and times?

Yes, we fully customize training dates to your operational schedule. Sessions can be scheduled during regular business hours, evenings, or Saturdays to prevent business interruption.

? How fast can we receive a proposal?

When you send an inquiry via our form, you will receive a transparent, non-binding proposal within 1 business day.
