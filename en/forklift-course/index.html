Frequently Asked Questions about Forklifts

? Is a forklift certificate legally mandatory in the Netherlands?

Yes. Under Article 7.17 of the Dutch Working Conditions Decree (Arbobesluit), operators of motorized mobile work equipment (forklifts, reach trucks) must possess demonstrably 'specific expertise'. With an official Edusafe certificate, your organization complies with labour inspections and insurance standards.

? How long is the forklift certificate valid?

The certificate and official ID card are valid for 5 years. After 5 years, the driver can take a concise half-day refresher course to extend validity for another 5 years.

? Are persons under 18 allowed to operate a forklift?

Individuals aged 16 and 17 who hold a certified certificate may operate a forklift provided there is expert supervision in the workplace. From age 18, operators can drive independently.

? Can you deliver the forklift training incompany at our warehouse?

Yes! Incompany forklift training is highly effective because staff are trained directly on your machinery, in your aisles, and with your actual inventory. From 4 participants, this is very cost-efficient.
